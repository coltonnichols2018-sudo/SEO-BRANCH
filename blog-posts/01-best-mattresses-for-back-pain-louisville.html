How Your Mattress Affects Back Pain

Your spine has a natural curve that needs to be supported while you sleep. When your mattress is too soft, your body sinks and your spine bends unnaturally. When it's too firm, your pressure points get compressed and your muscles can't fully relax.

The sweet spot is a mattress that keeps your spine aligned while cushioning your shoulders, hips, and knees. That "sweet spot" is different for everyone, which is why trying mattresses in person is so important.

Best Mattress Types for Back Pain

Memory Foam Mattresses

Best for: Side sleepers with lower back or hip pain

Memory foam contours to your body shape, filling in the gaps between your body and the mattress. This reduces pressure points and keeps your spine aligned. If you sleep on your side and wake up with hip or lower back soreness, memory foam is often the best choice.

Hybrid Mattresses

Best for: Back sleepers and combination sleepers

Hybrid mattresses combine innerspring coils with foam or latex layers on top. You get the support of coils with the comfort of foam. These are great if you move around a lot at night or sleep in multiple positions, because they're responsive and supportive at the same time.

Innerspring Mattresses

Best for: Stomach sleepers and people who prefer a firmer feel

Traditional innerspring mattresses offer firm, even support across the entire surface. If you sleep on your stomach, a firmer innerspring mattress helps prevent your midsection from sinking, which can strain your lower back.

Adjustable Bases

Best for: Chronic back pain, sciatica, and acid reflux

An adjustable base lets you elevate your head, feet, or both. Raising your knees slightly takes pressure off your lower back. Many of our Louisville customers with chronic back pain find that an adjustable base makes a bigger difference than the mattress itself.

Firmness Guide for Back Pain

Sleep Position Recommended Firmness Why
Side Sleeper Medium to Medium-Soft Cushions shoulders and hips to keep spine aligned
Back Sleeper Medium to Medium-Firm Supports the natural curve of your lower back
Stomach Sleeper Firm Prevents midsection from sinking and straining lower back
Combination Sleeper Medium Works across all positions as you move at night

Signs Your Current Mattress Is Causing Back Pain

  • You wake up stiff or sore but feel better after moving around for 15-30 minutes
  • Your mattress is more than 7-8 years old
  • You can see or feel sagging, lumps, or indentations
  • You sleep better in hotels or at other people's houses
  • You toss and turn all night trying to get comfortable

If any of these sound familiar, it's time to start shopping.
