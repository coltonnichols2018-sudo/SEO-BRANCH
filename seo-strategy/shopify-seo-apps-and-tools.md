## Bubba's Mattress & Furniture

## FREE / Essential Tools

### 1. Google Search Console (FREE - MUST HAVE)
- **What:** Monitor your site's presence in Google search results
- **Setup:** Go to search.google.com/search-console, add your domain, verify via Shopify
- **Actions:**
  - Submit your sitemap (bubbasmattressfurniturelouisville.com/sitemap.xml)
  - Monitor which keywords bring traffic
  - Find and fix crawl errors
  - See which pages are indexed

### 2. Google Analytics 4 (FREE - MUST HAVE)
- **What:** Track website visitors, behavior, and conversions
- **Setup:** Create GA4 property, add tracking code to Shopify
- **In Shopify:** Online Store > Preferences > Google Analytics

### 3. Google Business Profile (FREE - MUST HAVE)
- **What:** Your Google Maps listing
- **Setup:** business.google.com - claim and optimize your profile
- **See:** google-business-profile-optimization.md for full guide

### 4. Bing Webmaster Tools (FREE)
- **What:** Same as Search Console but for Bing/Yahoo searches
- **Setup:** bing.com/webmasters - submit your sitemap

## Recommended Shopify Apps

### SEO & Schema Markup
1. **SEO King** (Free plan available)
   - Auto-generates JSON-LD schema markup
   - Fixes meta tags, alt text
   - Sitemap optimization

2. **Smart SEO** ($9.99/month)
   - Auto-generates meta tags and alt text
   - Structured data (JSON-LD)
   - Sitemap management
   - Broken link detection

3. **Schema Plus for SEO** (Free plan available)
   - Adds rich snippet markup
   - Product, LocalBusiness, FAQ schema
   - Breadcrumb markup

### Page Speed
4. **Crush.pics** (Free plan available)
   - Automatically compresses images on upload
   - Faster page load = better SEO rankings

### Reviews
5. **Judge.me** (Free plan available)
   - Collect product reviews
   - Review schema markup (stars in Google results)
   - Email review requests automatically
   - Import reviews from other platforms

### Local SEO
6. **Store Locator / Map** (various free options)
   - Embed Google Maps on your site
   - Helps with local SEO signals

## Google Search Console - First Actions After Setup

1. **Submit Sitemap:**
   - URL: `https://www.bubbasmattressfurniturelouisville.com/sitemap.xml`
   - In Search Console: Sitemaps > Add new sitemap

2. **Request Indexing** of your most important pages:
   - Homepage
   - /collections/mattresses
   - /collections/living-room-recliners
   - /pages/about-us (create this first)
   - /pages/financing (create this first)

3. **Set Target Country:** United States
4. **Check Mobile Usability:** Fix any issues flagged
5. **Monitor Core Web Vitals:** Fix any "Poor" ratings

## Image SEO Checklist (Do This for ALL Product Images)

Every image uploaded to Shopify should have:
- [ ] Descriptive filename BEFORE uploading (e.g., `ashley-queen-mattress-louisville.jpg` not `IMG_4532.jpg`)
- [ ] Alt text with keyword + location (e.g., "Ashley Sleep queen memory foam mattress at Bubba's Mattress & Furniture Louisville KY")
- [ ] Compressed file size (under 200KB per image ideally)
- [ ] Appropriate dimensions (don't upload 4000px images for thumbnails)

### Alt Text Templates:
- Products: `[Brand] [Product Name] at Bubba's Mattress & Furniture Louisville KY`
- Store photos: `Bubba's Mattress & Furniture store [interior/exterior] at 5907 Terry Road Louisville KY`
- Category banners: `[Category] for sale at Bubba's Mattress & Furniture Louisville Kentucky`
