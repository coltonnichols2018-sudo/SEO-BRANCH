Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a credit check to qualify?›
Progressive, Acima, and Koalifi do not require a credit check. They base approval on your income and bank account. Synchrony requires a credit check.
How do I apply?›
You can apply online or in-store. Most approvals come back in seconds. Just pick the provider you want and complete a short application. You'll know your approval amount right away.
What if I pay it off early?›
All of our providers offer early payoff options. Progressive and Acima give you 90 days to pay it off with no interest. Koalifi gives you 100 days. The sooner you pay it off, the less you pay overall.
Will this affect my credit?›
Progressive and Acima do not report to credit bureaus. Koalifi reports to TransUnion, which can help build your credit with on-time payments. Synchrony reports to all major bureaus.
What do I need to apply?›
A valid ID, a bank account with direct deposits, and your Social Security number. That's it for the no-credit-needed options. Synchrony requires a standard credit application.
Can I use financing on any product?›
Yes. Financing is available on everything we carry — sectionals, sofas, mattresses, bedroom sets, dining, outdoor, all of it. No minimum purchase required.
