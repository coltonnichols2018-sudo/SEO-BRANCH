About Bubba's Mattress & Furniture in Louisville

Bubba's Mattress & Furniture is a locally-owned furniture store at 5907 Terry Road in Louisville, KY. We built this business on something the big chains will never have: a real connection to the Louisville community and a genuine commitment to helping families furnish their homes without the headaches.

Our Story

Rooted in Louisville and proud of it. We started Bubba's because we saw how the big-box stores treat people — like transactions instead of neighbors. We knew Louisville deserved better. When you shop at Bubba's, you get treated the way we'd want to be treated: with respect, patience, and straight talk about what's worth your money.

We partnered with Ashley Furniture because they make quality products that real families can afford. We carry the full Ashley lineup — sofas, sectionals, recliners, bedroom sets, mattresses, dining furniture, and outdoor pieces — and we stand behind everything we sell.

What Makes Us Different

  • We remember your name — Our customers come back because we build relationships, not just ring up sales
  • Straight answers on pricing — We'll tell you what something's worth and whether it's right for your situation
  • We deliver it ourselves — Our team handles delivery to your home, not a random third-party carrier
  • Real mattress advice — We'll ask about your sleep habits and recommend what actually helps, not what has the highest margin
  • Flexible payment options — Financing plans that work with your budget, not against it
