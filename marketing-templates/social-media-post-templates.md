## INSTAGRAM POST / REEL IDEAS

1. **"Tour our showroom"** — Walk through video showing mattresses, recliners, sofas
2. **"Mattress firmness test"** — Press on different mattresses showing soft vs firm
3. **"Before/After delivery"** — Empty room → furnished room (with customer permission)
4. **"Recliner demo"** — Show power recline, USB charging, rocking features
5. **"Finance tip"** — Quick graphic about how financing works
6. **"Did you know?"** — Quick facts about mattress care, furniture cleaning
7. **"Team intro"** — Short clips introducing each team member
8. **"New arrival unboxing"** — Unpack and set up new inventory on the showroom floor
9. **"Customer spotlight"** — Happy customer with their new furniture (with permission)
10. **"Louisville love"** — Content about the Louisville community, local events

| Day | Platform | Post Type |
|-----|----------|-----------|
| Monday | Google Business Profile | Product feature or tip |

| Wednesday | Instagram | Reel or photo (showroom, product demo) |
| Thursday | Google Business Profile | Sale, financing, or community post |
| Friday | Facebook | Weekend hours reminder + featured product |
| Saturday | Instagram | Story (behind the scenes, customer spotlight) |
| Sunday | — | Rest day (or schedule Monday's posts) |

**Minimum commitment:** 3 posts per week across platforms
**Ideal:** 5-7 posts per week with a mix of content types
