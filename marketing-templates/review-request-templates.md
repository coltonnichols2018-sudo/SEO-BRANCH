## Bubba's Mattress & Furniture

## HOW TO CREATE YOUR GOOGLE REVIEW LINK

1. Search for "Bubba's Mattress & Furniture" on Google
2. Click on your Google Business Profile
3. Click "Get more reviews" (or find the share review link)
4. Copy the link — it will look something like:
   `https://g.page/r/YOUR_PLACE_ID/review`
5. Use this link in ALL templates below (replace [YOUR GOOGLE REVIEW LINK])

## PRINTABLE QR CODE CARD

### Instructions:
1. Go to a free QR code generator (e.g., qr-code-generator.com)
2. Enter your Google Review link
3. Download the QR code image
4. Use the template below to create printed cards

- Checkout counter (next to the register)
- On delivery paperwork / receipts
- Business card holders in-store
- Taped to the inside of the front door (customers see it leaving)
- Attached to delivered furniture packaging
- In follow-up thank you notes

## RESPONDING TO GOOGLE REVIEWS

### Template: Positive Review Response
```
Thank you so much, [Name]! We loved helping you find the perfect
[product]. It's customers like you that make running a local
furniture store in Louisville so rewarding. Enjoy your new
[product] and don't hesitate to reach out if you need anything!
— The Bubba's Team
```

### Template: Negative Review Response
```
[Name], we're sorry to hear about your experience and we take
this feedback seriously. We'd love the chance to make it right.
Please give us a call at (502) 494-0376 so we can personally
address your concerns. We appreciate you letting us know.
— The Bubba's Team
```

### RULES FOR REVIEW RESPONSES:
1. Respond to EVERY review within 24 hours
2. Always use the customer's name
3. Always be professional and thankful, even with negative reviews
4. Never argue in public — take it offline via phone
5. Mention specific products when possible (shows you're paying attention)
6. Include "Louisville" naturally in responses (helps local SEO)
